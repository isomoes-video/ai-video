# Source and editorial scope

Source: https://latent.space/p/ai-is-getting-good-at-math/
Title: AI Is Getting Good at Math. Here’s Why.
Author: Ankit Maloo · Latent Space
Retrieved: 2026-10-29

This Chinese adaptation explains the author's conceptual framework: ordered hints, work between insights, search costs, automated verification, negative hints, and human–AI collaboration.

The article's breadth-first/depth-first contrast is a simplifying analogy, not a universal characterization of humans or LLM algorithms. Its complexity expressions are heuristics, not measured laws. The reconstructed unit-distance workflow is the author's interpretation, not an observed execution trace. Claims about individual recent mathematical breakthroughs, compute budgets, and the relativity thought experiment were not independently verified and are not presented here as established results.
